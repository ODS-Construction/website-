After you send it

What happens next

  1. 01We get in touch to ask a few questions, usually by phone, sometimes by email
  2. 02We come and walk the property with you, at no charge
  3. 03You get a written, line-item estimate separating materials from labour
  4. 04We go through it with you and answer whatever comes up
  5. 05If you decide to build, we set a start date and begin ordering long-lead items

Nothing is committed until you say so, and there's no cost to you up to that point.

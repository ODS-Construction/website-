Who we are

ODS Construction is a family-run builder working the length of Eleuthera. We build new homes, renovate and extend existing ones, put in concrete pools, replace roofs, build docks, and maintain properties for owners who aren't on the island year-round.

Most of our clients are American, British and European owners building or keeping a house here. Many of them are several thousand miles away for much of the year, which shapes how we run a job more than anything else does.

Finish carpentry, self-performed

We do the work ourselves

Most contractors this size are really coordinators — they win the job and then hand each piece to whoever is available. We don't work that way. Our own crews do the structural work, the plumbing, the mechanical rough-in, the tile and the finish carpentry.

It's the single biggest reason our schedules hold. When the crew works for us, the sequence is ours to control, the standard is ours to set, and there's one company answering for the result instead of four pointing at each other.

What twenty‑six years here teaches you

Experience on this island is mostly specific knowledge, and none of it transfers from the mainland. Which sites take a truck and which take a barge. How long a container really takes out of Miami. Which suppliers deliver and which will leave you waiting. What salt does to the wrong fixing over five years. Where the rock starts.

It's unglamorous, and it's the whole difference between a schedule you can plan a flight around and one that keeps slipping.

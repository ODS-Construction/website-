About ODS Construction

ODS Construction has been building on Eleuthera for 26 years. We build custom homes, renovate and extend existing ones, put in concrete pools, replace roofs and maintain properties for owners who aren't on the island year-round.

Building here is not the same as building on the mainland. Almost every material crosses water before it reaches your site, salt and storm season set the standard for how things get detailed, and there is no supply house down the road when something is short. We plan around that instead of discovering it halfway through a job.

We also self-perform most of what other contractors hand off (structure, plumbing, mechanical rough-in, tile and finish work), which is why our schedules hold and our sites stay tidy. The owner is on site, not on a call from somewhere else.

Our process

How We Work

Every ODS job runs the same five steps, whether it's a bathroom or a whole house. It exists so you always know what stage you're at, what it costs, and what happens next, and so nothing gets ordered, poured or closed up before it should be.

  1. 01

    Site Visit & Scope

    We walk the property with you, look at access, ground conditions, power and water, and talk through what you actually want out of the space. No charge, and no obligation to build.

  2. 02

    Detailed Written Estimate

    You get a written estimate that separates materials from labour and carries the landed cost of imported materials (freight, duty and VAT) in the numbers from the start. We price the job in full detail internally, trade by trade, and present it to you clearly. What you see is what the job costs, not a figure that grows once the containers land.

  3. 03

    Procurement & Scheduling

    We identify the long-lead items first (windows, doors, roofing, pool and mechanical equipment) and order them early through our South Florida suppliers, shipping into Eleuthera on consolidated freight. The build schedule is then sequenced around those arrivals rather than against them.

  4. 04

    Construction

    Our own crews self-perform the structural, mechanical, plumbing and finish work, under daily site supervision. You get regular progress photographs and a straight answer about where the job stands, whether you're on the island or not.

  5. 05

    Handover & Follow-Up

    We walk the finished work with you, close out the punch list, hand over the documentation and equipment manuals, and stay reachable afterwards. Most of our work comes from owners we've already built for.

From the first site walk to the last punch item, it's the same team on your job.
Eleuthera & the Out Islands

Where We Build

ODS Construction is based in Palmetto Point and works the length of Eleuthera: Governors Harbour, Windermere Island, Rock Sound, Tarpum Bay, Hatchet Bay, Gregory Town and Harbour Island.

Being based here matters more than it sounds. We know which sites take a truck and which take a barge, which stretches of road will cost you a day, how long a container really takes from Miami, and which suppliers actually deliver. That knowledge is the difference between a schedule you can plan a flight around and one that keeps slipping.

If it can be built on this island, we know what it takes to build it.
